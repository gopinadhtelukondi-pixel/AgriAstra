# Cotton production practices: Telangana

Issuing organizations: Cotton Research Station, Warangal and Adilabad; Professor Jayashankar Telangana State Agricultural University (PJTSAU). Publication prepared in 2023 and hosted by ICAR-Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR).
Source: https://www.cicr.org.in/wp-content/uploads/POP-TS-01.pdf

## Scope

This guide is specific to Telangana. It is a concise summary of the state package, not a complete crop plan. Follow the current recommendation for the farmer's district, crop stage, variety, soil, and water availability. Do not apply pesticide or fertilizer rates from this summary.

## Practices to review when improving cotton productivity

- Choose a variety or hybrid recommended for the specific location. The source advises selecting hybrids based on their performance in that location.
- Follow the Telangana sowing window and local rainfall guidance; do not copy dates from another state or season.
- Consider short-duration intercrops such as green gram, black gram, cowpea, or soybean where the local extension recommendation and field layout support them.
- The source highlights supportive irrigation during boll formation and development when prolonged drought occurs. Whether this applies depends on actual crop stage and water availability.
- Use the local soil and crop recommendation to plan nutrients. This package has separate guidance for varieties and hybrids; the right rate cannot be inferred without knowing which is grown and the current state recommendation.
- Keep weeds under timely control using methods approved for the crop stage and local conditions.
- Use integrated pest monitoring approaches such as border/intercrops, trap crops, pheromone traps, and locally recommended botanical options. Identify the pest and check a current expert recommendation before any pesticide use.

## Limits

These practices do not predict this field's yield. The source includes specific input rates and pesticide options that are intentionally not repeated here. Ask the local KVK or agriculture officer for current, crop-stage-specific rates and treatment advice.
